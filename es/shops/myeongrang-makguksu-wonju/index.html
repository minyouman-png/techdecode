Myeongrang Makguksu

Cold buckwheat noodles and sliced pork, in the back lanes beside Wonju’s old station

Verificado2026-08-10
📞 Llamar033-742-6805🗺 지도

Novedades

  • 2026-08-10Hours are listed as 11:00–19:30 as of this check. No closing day is listed anywhere, so call ahead.Fuente: DiningCode ↗

Wonju Station moved to Musil-dong in 2021. The Hakseong-dong district around the old station went quiet after the trains stopped, but the shops that were there before are still there. This is one of them.

The menu runs from makguksu (cold buckwheat noodles) and pyeonyuk (sliced boiled pork) through to whole-chicken and duck pots. DiningCode files it under “old-timer” and “where locals go” — it sits on a neighbourhood route, not a tourist one.

It is recorded as having appeared on the Korean TV programmes “Life’s Master” and “Choija Road”. Kakao Map rates it 4.5 from 72 ratings; DiningCode 4.4.
