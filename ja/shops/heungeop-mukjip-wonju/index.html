Heungeop-myeon sits south of central Wonju — a place you pass through rather than visit. This shop works almost entirely in buckwheat jelly.

Muk is old Gangwon food: buckwheat ground, cooked to a set, then sliced into broth or dressed as a salad. With so few ingredients, there is nowhere to hide.

DiningCode rates it 4.5 from 8, Kakao Map 4.2 from 30. Hygiene scores 5.0. It is recorded as having appeared on the TV programme “Life’s Master”.
