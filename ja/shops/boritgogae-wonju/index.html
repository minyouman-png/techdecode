Banggok-dong is the newest part of Wonju — new buildings, chain outlets. Tucked inside the ground floor of H Tower is a place serving barley rice the old way.

Barley rice was what people ate when rice was short; the shop is named after that period. Today it arrives as a set with stews, pancakes and a spread of vegetable sides.

Both review platforms agree, which is rare: 4.6 from 31 on DiningCode, 4.4 from 259 on Kakao Map. Value for money scores 4.9 — higher than the food score itself.
