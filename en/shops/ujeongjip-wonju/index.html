There are several snack stalls in the basement of Jayu Market in central Wonju. Ujeongjip is one of them, tucked inside the lower floor.

The menu is short: rice cakes, blood sausage, fritters, seaweed rolls. A portion of tteokbokki is ₩4,000, a seaweed roll ₩2,500, fritters ₩1,000 each — market prices, more or less unchanged.

DiningCode rates it 4.5 from 5, Kakao Map 4.1 from 24. The most common descriptor on DiningCode is “where locals go”.
