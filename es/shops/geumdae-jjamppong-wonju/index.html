Geumdae Jjamppong

A Korean-Chinese kitchen with a wide yard, on the road to Geumdae valley

Geumdae-ri sits on the southern skirt of Chiaksan, on the way into Geumdae valley. You need a car to get here.

It is a Korean-Chinese restaurant, but not the kind found in a city arcade — the listing tags are “valley”, “terrace”, “wide yard”. A stream runs below the restaurant.

DiningCode rates it 4.8 from 4 and Kakao Map 4.6 from 14 — high on both, but from a small sample.
